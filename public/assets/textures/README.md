# Original canopy textures

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this campus project.
The supplied benchmark informed material richness; no game assets were copied.

`campus-foliage.png`: seamless painted overlapping tropical leaves, emerald
and lime variations, fine central veins, even diffuse illumination, no scene,
text, borders or directional shadows.

`campus-blossoms.png`: seamless painted clusters of small five-petal pink,
blush and rose flowers with yellow centers, sparse green leaves and fine
twigs, even diffuse illumination, no scene, text or borders.

Full generation prompts are preserved in `prompts.json` in this directory.
